# Vrinda Store Annual Dashboard

## 📊 Description

This project presents a comprehensive **Power BI dashboard** visualizing sales and order data from the Vrinda online store.
The dashboard provides insights into customer demographics, top-performing regions, sales channels, and monthly trends.

- **DASHBOARD.png**  
  A screenshot of the final dashboard, capturing all visualizations and filters used.

---

##  Features

- **Monthly Sales vs. Orders**  
  Analyze how orders and revenue trend across different months of the year.

- **Top 5 States by Sales**  
  Identify the states with the highest sales contribution (e.g., Maharashtra, Karnataka, etc.).

- **Sales by Gender**  
  See the split between male and female customer purchases.

- **Order Status Breakdown**  
  Pie chart showing percentage of delivered, returned, refunded, and cancelled orders.

- **Orders by Age Group and Gender**  
  Explore the purchasing behavior of teenagers, adults, and seniors by gender.

- **Sales Channels Analysis**  
  Evaluate the contribution of each platform like Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Meesho, etc.

---

## 🛠 Technologies Used

- **Power BI** – for dashboard development and interactive visualizations.
- **Excel/CSV** – for cleaning and handling sales data.

---

## 🔍 Insights

- **Women** contributed to **64%** of total sales.
- **Amazon and Flipkart** were the top-performing sales channels.
- **Maharashtra** led in overall sales volume.
- The majority of orders were **delivered successfully (92%)**.
